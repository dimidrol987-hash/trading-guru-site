01 Sound familiar?

Plenty of trades. No clue why you're losing

Most traders go in circles not because “the method doesn't work”, but because they can't see what exactly they're doing wrong.

“Hit a stop — so the method is bad”

A week later you can't remember where you entered or why you left. The verdict is ready: change the method and add indicators. The circle starts again.

“A green month — I'm doing great”

Inside, one method made money and another ate half of it. The total is green, so you keep trading the second method.

“Raised my risk after a stop and won it back”

The money came back — so you did it right? No. That was luck. Next time, with that risk, it may not come back.

Trading Guru closes the loop: system → validation → honest review → correction. Every trade becomes material for growth.

12 FAQ

Is it really free for 7 days?
Yes. Download, install and use every feature for 7 days without limits — no card, no sign-up. Like it — choose 6 months or a year. Don't — just uninstall.
How do I pay?
Message @TradingGuru_pro in Telegram — I'll send the payment details. We accept USDT. After payment you get a key: enter it in the app (Settings → “License and activation”) and the full version unlocks. No subscriptions, no auto-renewals.
I'm a beginner without a system. Will the app help?
Yes — that's exactly what the trading-system wizard is for. It walks you through 8 steps, explains why each one matters and won't let you skip the important parts, like the stop or filters. Then you test the system in the simulator on history, not with your own money.
What is the bar-by-bar simulator?
The app takes a random piece of real history and hides the dates so you don't know what comes next. You move the chart candle by candle and make decisions as in real trading. Trades close at the stop and take by themselves and go into a practice journal — separate from the real one.
Which exchanges and terminals are supported?
Trade import via API (read-only): Binance, Bybit, OKX, BitGet, plus MEXC for spot. MetaTrader 4 and 5 — via the account history report (HTML). The risk calculator handles Moscow Exchange stocks and futures, currencies, crypto and forex. Trades from other venues can be added manually.
Which devices? Do I need the internet?
A desktop app for Windows 10/11. For full use you need the internet: charts and quotes, trade import, the simulator, the Telegram bot and mentor connection. Without the internet the journal, risk calculator and checklists still open — you add trades manually then. The trades themselves are stored on your computer.
Are my trades and exchange keys safe?
Your data is stored on your computer. Exchange keys are used only to read history and positions and never leave the app. No accounts are required. The app may send anonymous usage statistics — a random install number and which steps were completed, no trades, keys or names; you can turn it off in settings.
Is the app available in English?
Yes. The interface, hints, trainer, guide, AI reviews and the coach's voice are available in English and Russian. The language follows your system and can be changed in Settings → “View” → “Language”.
Windows warns me during installation — is that dangerous?
No. The installer doesn't have a paid code signature yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”.
I'm a mentor or a school. How do I connect students?
Install the app and open Settings → “Mentor cabinet” → “Become a mentor”. 7 days and up to 3 students are free. Students who have the app connect with your code; those who don't, with a key you issue them in the cabinet (included in the plan). Every mentor sees only their own students.
What happens to students when the mentor's plan ends?
Students who got keys from a mentor have access while the mentor's plan is active. When the mentor renews, the students' access renews by itself. When training ends, a student can buy the app with 10% off using the mentor's code or become a mentor. The journal and all data stay with the student either way.
Are there discounts?
Yes. 10% off with a friend's or mentor's code. A friend who already has Trading Guru (it's under the 🎁 icon at the top of the app) or your mentor gives it to you. Enter it when buying: “Buy the full version” → “Have a friend's code?”. After you buy, you get your own code: friends get a discount and you get 10% of their purchases.
